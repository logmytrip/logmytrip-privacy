Log My Trip: Privacy Policy

App: Log My Trip
Developer: Preetam Roy
Contact: support.logmytrip@gmail.com
Effective date: November 14, 2026

The short version

Log My Trip keeps your trip log on your phone. We do not run a server for your data, we do not ask you to create an account, and the app does not use GPS or location. We cannot see your trips.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your trips, vehicles and settings (dates, odometer readings, destinations, purposes, notes). These are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Purchase status (whether you have a plan). This is stored on your phone and confirmed with Google Play.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.
  • It does not include advertising or analytics tools.
  • It does not sell or share personal information.

Things you choose to do

  • Export a PDF or CSV: the file is created on your phone and handed to the app you pick in your phone's share menu (for example email or Drive). From that point, that app's privacy policy applies.
  • Back up your data: the backup is a file you save or share yourself. It contains your travel history, so keep it private. Purchase status is not included in backups.
  • Share the app with a friend: the share message does not contain any of your data.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. The payment company processes your payment details; we never see them. We receive only the confirmation that a purchase is active.

Reminders

If you turn on the weekly reminder, the app schedules a notification on your phone at the day and time you choose. It is created and shown on the device only. It never contains destinations or distances, and nothing is sent to us.

Android automatic backup

Android's cloud auto-backup is turned off for this app, so your trip log is not uploaded to your Google account unless you make a backup file yourself.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored in it. Backup and export files you created are yours to delete.

Children

The app is meant for adults who keep mileage records for work. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for meaningful changes, tell you in the app.
